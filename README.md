# Spending Notebook

A one-page app for logging daily expenses and income, with a monthly budget (base + meal card), category and payment breakdowns, and a day-by-day ledger.

**Live:** https://pedromfgonc-cmyk.github.io/spending-notebook/

## Where the data lives

Everything is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. Nothing is sent to a server. Entries made on your phone don't appear on your computer, and clearing the browser's site data erases them.

Use **Download backup** at the bottom of the page now and then. **Restore from backup** brings a backup into any browser, and skips entries that are already there.

## Hosting

Served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root. Any change pushed to `index.html` is live about a minute later.
